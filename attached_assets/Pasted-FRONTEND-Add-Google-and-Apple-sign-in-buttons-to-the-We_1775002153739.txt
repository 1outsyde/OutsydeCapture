THE PROBLEM:
Google and Apple sign-in buttons exist on Slide 4 of onboarding but are completely absent from the Sign In screen. A returning user who originally signed up with Google has no way to sign back in. Both screens need these buttons — they serve different users:

Slide 4 → new users choosing how to sign UP
Sign In screen → returning users signing back IN


THE FIX:
Step 1 — Move handleGoogleSignIn and handleAppleSignIn to a shared location
If these functions are currently defined inside OnboardingScreen, move them to AuthContext or a shared useGoogleSignIn / useAppleSignIn hook so both screens can call them without duplicating code. If they're already in AuthContext, simply import and use them on both screens.
Do not copy-paste the handler logic into two places — one source of truth only.
Step 2 — Add the buttons to the Sign In screen
On the "Welcome back" Sign In screen, add Google and Apple buttons in this exact layout:
[ Sign In ]                    ← existing blue button, unchanged

Don't have an account? Sign up ← existing, unchanged

──────── or ────────           ← existing divider, unchanged

[ G  Continue with Google ]    ← ADD THIS

──────── or ────────

[ Continue as Guest ]          ← existing, unchanged
Button styles must match the buttons on Slide 4 exactly — same height, same border radius, same font, same colors:

Google button: dark green background (#1A4A1A), white text, envelope or Google icon on left
Apple button: black background (#000000), white text, Apple/phone icon on left

Step 3 — Update the Email field to accept username too
While on this screen, update the identifier field:

Label: "Email or Username *"
Placeholder: "your@email.com or @username"
Detection logic on submit:

javascriptconst isEmail = identifier.includes('@') && identifier.includes('.');
